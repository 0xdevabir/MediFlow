# T01: Identity, RBAC, MFA, consent, break-glass (M7): report

Status: code complete on `feat/T01-identity-rbac`. **No tests were run for the final state.** Per the coordinator's instruction, `./mvnw verify` was skipped and Docker was never started. `./mvnw -q -DskipTests test-compile` is green.

## What was built (all under `com.mediflow.identity`)
- **Flyway `V100__identity_security.sql`:** `identity_login_state`, `identity_password_reset_token`, `identity_break_glass_review`, `identity_referral_share`. The V1 tables `mfa_secret`, `break_glass_access` and `consent` are reused.
- **access/:** the RBAC matrix and object-level checks.
  - `AccessMatrix` implements R&D v1.3 §12. Where rd.txt and tech.txt differ, rd.txt wins: nurse demographics R*, nurse vitals R C, front-desk direct-test orders C⁶, patient lab orders R C⁶.
  - `AccessPolicy` is the `@access` bean.
  - `JdbcAccessFacts` and `IdentityPatientAccessPort` (the real `PatientAccessPort`).
  - Assignment means: a checked-in appointment or encounter with that doctor today (Asia/Dhaka), an active referral share, or active break-glass. For ER, an active red alert also counts.
  - Lab techs see patients with an open lab order (or one verified in the last 24 h). Pharmacists see patients with a signed prescription in the dispense window. Nurses and front desk have staff scope.
- **login/:**
  - `IdentityAuthenticationProvider` adds lockout to the form login: 5 failures lock the account for 15 minutes (`LockoutPolicy`, `LoginAttemptService`).
  - Logins are audited as `LOGIN_SUCCESS`, `LOGIN_FAILED` and `LOGIN_LOCKED`.
  - Unknown usernames do not create rows.
- **password/:**
  - `PasswordPolicy`: at least 12 characters, at least 3 of the 4 character classes, not containing the username, not on a common-password list.
  - `PasswordHasher`: Argon2id when BouncyCastle is present, otherwise BCrypt (see deviations).
  - `PasswordResetService` and controller, on a public filter chain (`IdentityPublicSecurityConfig`).
    - The token is 32 random bytes, stored as SHA-256 only, valid for 30 minutes, single-use.
    - Older tokens are invalidated when a new one is issued.
    - Requests for unknown users give the same response as known ones.
    - A successful reset also clears the lockout.
- **mfa/:** TOTP, with mandatory MFA for DOCTOR, PHARMACIST, ADMIN and CLINICAL_APPROVER.
  - `TotpService`: SHA1, 6 digits, 30 s, ±1 step, QR as a data URI. The secret is AES-GCM encrypted at rest. A code from an already-used step is rejected (replay protection).
  - `MfaService` enrol/verify/reset. Wrong codes count toward lockout.
  - `MfaGateFilter` and `MfaRequirementResolver`: until MFA is done, every request goes to enrol or verify; `/api/**` gets 401.
  - `MfaController`: `/identity/mfa/enrol`, `/verify` and `/step-up`. The session id rotates after verification. A lockout invalidates the session.
  - `MfaReauthService.requireFreshMfa()` (5-minute window) and `MfaWebAdvice`.
- **breakglass/:** `BreakGlassService` and controller.
  - The reason must be 10–1000 characters; access lasts 60 minutes.
  - Activating again while access is active does not extend it.
  - Each activation is audited and notifies every admin by IN_APP and EMAIL.
  - Admins get a review list (MRN only) and record one review per access: JUSTIFIED or NOT_JUSTIFIED, with a note.
- **consent/:**
  - `ConsentService`: record, check active, revoke, list.
  - `ReferralShareService`: time-limited sharing with another clinician (1 hour to 30 days, default 7 days). It requires the patient's agreement and is revocable by the granter, the patient or an admin.
  - `SharingController` at `/identity/sharing`.
- **staff/:** `StaffUserService` for T11 (ADMIN-only). It creates users, assigns roles, links or creates a practitioner, enables/disables, resets MFA, unlocks and lists. It blocks self-demotion and self-disable. Email and phone are encrypted.
- **UI:** templates `identity/{mfa-enrol,mfa-verify,password-forgot,password-reset,break-glass-new,break-glass-review,sharing}.html`; messages `identity_{en,bn}.properties`.

## Deviations
1. **BCrypt fallback:** BouncyCastle is not on the classpath and `pom.xml` is out of scope, so hashing falls back to BCrypt. T00's `PasswordEncoder` bean is still BCrypt (request T00-1).
2. **ER assignment:** any ER user counts as assigned to any patient with an active red alert, because there is no assignee column (request to T04).
3. **Referral-consent label:** the consent is stored as `DATA_PROCESSING` with `REFERRAL_SHARE/v1` (request T00-3).
4. **MFA off in dev by default:** this keeps T00's seeded-login IT working. `IdentityMfaIT` turns it on explicitly.
5. **MFA wiring:** MFA runs as a servlet filter after the security chain, plus a separate public filter chain for password pages. T00's `SecurityConfig` was not edited.
6. **Patient record-access view:** "Who accessed my record" is not built (owned by T11).
