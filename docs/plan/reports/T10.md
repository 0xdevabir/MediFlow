# T10 report: Notifications & reminders (M8)

Branch: `feat/T10-notifications`. Code: `com.mediflow.notification/**`, `templates/notification/**`, `messages/notification_{en,bn}.properties`, `V500__notification_module.sql`.

## Built
- **NotificationPort implementation.** `NotificationService` is a `@Service`, so the T00 logging stub backs off; a test checks this. `send(Request)` renders a template for the event in the recipient's language and fans it out. Language comes from the preference, then `app_user.locale`, then `en`.
- **Channels.**
  - SMS: the `SmsGateway` interface; `LoggingSmsGateway` (dev, masked number); `HttpSmsGateway` for a BD bulk-SMS HTTP API (`provider=http`).
  - Email: Spring Mail. It only logs when there is no `JavaMailSender`.
  - Web push: `PushGateway`; `LoggingPushGateway` in dev; `FcmPushGateway` uses FCM v1 (`provider=fcm`); tokens are registered at `POST/DELETE /api/notifications/push-tokens`.
  - In-app: inbox page plus a bell fragment.
- **Async delivery** (`NotificationDispatcher`). With a RabbitMQ `ConnectionFactory`:
  - Direct exchange `notify` with work queues `notify.push|sms|email|in_app`.
  - Retries go through a TTL'd `notify.retry` queue that dead-letters back to the work queues, with exponential backoff (2s ×2, capped at 5m, 3 attempts).
  - Final failures also go to `notify.dlq`.
  - Without Rabbit (tests, dev without a broker), delivery runs in-memory after commit, with retries on a small scheduler. A zero delay runs inline.
  - If a publish fails, delivery falls back to in-process.
- **Escalation and staff call list (R7).** After the final failure:
  - The notification is marked FAILED and a copy is created on the next reachable channel (PUSH → SMS → EMAIL → IN_APP), linked by `root_notification_id` and `escalated_to_id`.
  - Any final external failure, or a chain with nowhere to go, adds an entry to `staff_call_entry` (one per chain), shown at `/notifications/call-list` for FRONT_DESK/NURSE/ER/ADMIN. Viewing and resolving the list is audited.
  - Critical lab results and Red Alerts with no staff recipients always get a call entry.
- **Templates** for every event, in `bn` and `en`.
- **Listeners** (`@TransactionalEventListener`, own transaction, failures logged):
  - PatientRegistered → welcome.
  - AppointmentBooked → patient and doctor, plus 24h and 2h reminders.
  - AppointmentCancelled → patient and doctor, and its reminders are cancelled.
  - AppointmentCheckedIn → token message, and visit reminders are cancelled.
  - TriageLevelSet URGENT/CRITICAL → HIGH alert to the doctor.
  - RedAlertRaised → HIGH in-app/push to all ER and nurse users and to doctors with an active role at the hospital.
  - LabOrderCreated → patient.
  - LabReportVerified: normal → report ready (patient and ordering doctor); abnormal → "see a doctor"; critical → HIGH SMS to the patient, HIGH alert to the ordering and duty doctors, and a call entry.
  - PrescriptionSigned → message plus dose reminders.
  - PrescriptionAmended → reschedule.
  - PrescriptionRevoked → cancel reminders plus a HIGH message.
  - MedicationDispensed → patient.
- **Reminder scheduler.**
  - `DoseScheduleCalculator` handles OD/BD/TDS/QID/HS, the `1+0+1` notation, QnH and Bangla words and digits × days, weeks, months or "continue" (capped at 30 days). PRN, SOS, STAT and unparseable input produce no reminders.
  - A `@Scheduled` job (`NotificationSchedulingConfig`, default every 60s) sends due reminders using `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, so it is safe across several instances.
  - Follow-up reminders are created by `scheduleFollowUp`: the day before at 18:00 and the day itself at 08:00.
- **Adherence.**
  - Patients acknowledge doses as TAKEN or SKIPPED (`/notifications/reminders`, `adherence_log`). A dose can be acknowledged up to 1h early; re-acknowledging overwrites.
  - Adherence % = taken ÷ doses due (excluding cancelled).
  - It is shown on the reminders page and in the `notification/fragments :: adherence` widget for the patient dashboard.
- **Preferences** (`/notifications/preferences`): per-channel on/off, quiet hours (wrap past midnight; NORMAL messages keep only in-app, HIGH ignores quiet hours) and language. Changes are audited.

## Deviations
- `ReminderService` writes T00's `reminder` table and keeps the reference to its source (Rx, appointment or follow-up) in `payload.sourceId`, with a JSONB expression index in V500. No column was added to the shared entity.
- Notification metadata (event type, priority, attempts, escalation, read state) lives in T10's `notification_delivery` table, beside the shared `notification` row.
- Staff contacts are reached only by in-app and push. `app_user` email and phone are encrypted columns owned by T01 and are not decrypted here. Patients use the `patient` record (phone and email).
- "Duty doctor" is approximated as doctors with an active `practitioner_role` at the hospital. There is no roster yet.
